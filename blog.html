A week above the clouds

Our Swiss adventure story

There are trips you remember by dates, and there are trips you remember by a feeling. Switzerland gave us cold mornings, long paths and the sort of silence that makes every sound feel important.

We arrived in Zermatt before the streets were busy. From the station, the mountain appeared between the roofs for only a second, then disappeared behind the clouds. That brief view was enough to set the pace for the whole week: walk slowly, look twice and never assume the best moment has already passed.

One of the best hikes we’ve done so far — not because it was the longest, but because every turn changed the view.

The route we would walk again

Our favorite day started beside the forest and climbed toward the cable-car station. The route was clear, but the weather changed quickly. We kept an extra layer close, stopped for water before every steep section and left enough time to return before dusk.

The practical details mattered: comfortable boots, offline maps and a flexible plan. Still, the strongest memory is simple — eating lunch on a quiet ridge while the clouds moved below us.
