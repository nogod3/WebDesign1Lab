Shanghai grand final

Spirit vs VISION: the final chapter

The International 2026 brought sixteen of the world’s best teams to Shanghai. After the group stage and a double-elimination main event, only Team Spirit and TEAM VISION remained.

The grand final lasted all five games. VISION pushed the series to its limit, but Team Spirit closed the deciding map and claimed the Aegis with a 3–2 score.

The Aegis is claimed only after the last Ancient falls.

The route we would walk again

Our favorite day started beside the forest and climbed toward the cable-car station. The route was clear, but the weather changed quickly. We kept an extra layer close, stopped for water before every steep section and left enough time to return before dusk.

The practical details mattered: comfortable boots, offline maps and a flexible plan. Still, the strongest memory is simple — eating lunch on a quiet ridge while the clouds moved below us.
