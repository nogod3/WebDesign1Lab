Shanghai grand final

Spirit vs VISION: the final chapter

The International 2026 brought sixteen of the world’s best teams to Shanghai. After the group stage and a double-elimination main event, only Team Spirit and TEAM VISION remained.

The grand final lasted all five games. VISION pushed the series to its limit, but Team Spirit closed the deciding map and claimed the Aegis with a 3–2 score.

The Aegis is claimed only after the last Ancient falls.

How the final was decided

Team Spirit reached the deciding series through the lower bracket, while TEAM VISION arrived after winning the upper-bracket final. The different routes produced the closest possible finish: neither finalist could build a lead larger than one game.

The tournament began with sixteen teams. The group stage ran from August 13 to 16, followed by the main event from August 20 to 23 at the SPD Bank Oriental Sports Center. Spirit’s last victory turned four days of arena play into another championship.
